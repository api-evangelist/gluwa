The partnership aims to harness blockchain technology to enhance financial inclusion, improve eNaira functionality, and foster financial innovation. We are thrilled to share monumental news! After years of relentless effort, Gluwa has signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with the Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN) to officially onboard as a Partner Agent and help drive the increased adoption of Nigeria’s CBDC, also known as eNaira.
