Nigeria, home to over 200 million people, has one of the youngest populations globally, with more than 60% under the age of 25. This youthful demographic offers unique opportunities for tech adoption, workforce training, infrastructure development, and economic growth. Nigeria’s economy, projected by the World Bank to grow by 2.9% in 2024, stands to benefit immensely from a tech-savvy workforce.
